MANIFESTO

Tuntematon is Finnish word for "Unknown".

I am exactly that unknown artist. I am no one. No one has heard of me — though many have likely seen my work without even realizing it.

My work does not announce itself. It hides in plain sight: You may have passed it, stepped on it, mistook it for someone else's creation (and vice versa), dismissed it as maintenance debris or civic carelessness. That is not failure but the condition of its visibility.

I work in the fault lines between binaries that structure our perception: yes/no, clear/unclear, prominent/unknown, contemporary/archaic, simple/complex, art/not art.

These categories organize our world, but they are not stable. A single gesture can belong to both sides at once, or to neither. My practice is an attempt to occupy that gap — to produce objects and actions that resist easy classification, that remain illegible, suspicious and even wrong.
